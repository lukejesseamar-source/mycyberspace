About

Who I am and what I care about

I'm a writer and builder interested in the intersection of technology, craft, and intentional living. This site is my digital garden—a place to cultivate ideas, share projects, and document what I'm learning along the way.

My work tends to gravitate toward simplicity and sustainability. Whether I'm writing an essay or building a web application, I'm drawn to approaches that strip away the unnecessary and focus on what matters. There's something deeply satisfying about creating things that are both functional and beautiful in their restraint.

What I'm Focused On

  • Building small, focused web applications that solve real problems
  • Writing essays that explore technology, philosophy, and daily practice
  • Learning to code better, write clearer, and think deeper
  • Maintaining a sustainable creative practice in an age of distraction

How I Work

I believe in the power of consistency over intensity. Rather than sporadic bursts of productivity, I prefer steady, deliberate progress. Most of what I create comes from daily habits—writing in the morning, coding in the afternoon, reading in the evening. Simple rhythms that compound over time.

This site itself is an ongoing project, evolving as I do. It's not meant to be perfect or complete, just honest and current. A reflection of where I am and what I'm thinking about right now.
